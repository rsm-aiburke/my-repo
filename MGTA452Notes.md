# Lecture 1

## Request

Request contains 3 things

- Reported Pattern
- Proposed Explanation
- Proposed Action

Ask what prompted the request and when a choice is needed

## Decision outline: make choice explicit

Purpose: agree with the decision the evidence must support

Clarify with manager:

Who chooses?

What are the alternatives?

What should improve?

What counts as success?

What could go wrong?

Which customers and periods?

What are the limits?

## Questions

Four kinds

- descriptive: looking at the past to establish what happened
- predictive: Anticipate an outcome using previous data
- causal: Estimate what action causes a certain change
- prescriptive: Choose under goals and limits
